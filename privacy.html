Privacy Policy

Application: Mini Personal Finance · Effective date: 10 October 2026

This application is a private, self-hosted personal finance tool used by one individual to record, import and review personal financial information.

Information processed

The application may process account names, balances, transaction dates, amounts, descriptions, categories, recurring items, budgets and documents that the owner deliberately enters or imports.

When an open-banking connection is enabled, the selected account provider and its authorised banking intermediary may process the account information required to provide balances and transactions. The owner completes the bank consent directly with the relevant provider.

Where information is stored

Application data, credentials, access tokens, imported files and backups are stored in the owner-controlled private deployment. Database access is restricted to the application services. No financial information is sold, rented or shared for advertising.

Purpose and legal basis

Information is processed solely to maintain the owner's personal ledger, reconcile accounts, produce reports and import authorised account information. The legal basis is the owner's request and consent for the selected connections.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained until the owner deletes it or removes the relevant backup. Open-banking consent can be revoked with the account provider. Revoking consent stops future access; it does not automatically delete records already imported into the private ledger.

Security

Access is restricted to authenticated users and private service credentials. Secrets are kept outside public source files. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection, so backups and access credentials must be protected by the owner.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application or its data flows change. The effective date above will be updated with material changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the owner through the contact details associated with the application registration.
